This article addresses why legitimate emails land in spam folders, noting that one in six real emails never reach inboxes. It covers three key factors: infrastructure (authentication records), domain reputation, and content engagement, and recommends implementing SPF, DKIM, and DMARC to establish sender legitimacy.
